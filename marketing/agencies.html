Agency questions

How does Scheduleistic help agencies?

Scheduleistic gives agencies separate workspaces per client, client approval workflows, team roles, and white-label basics, so you can manage many clients from one organized account.

Is there a white-label social media scheduler option?

Yes. White-label basics are available on the Agency plan so client-facing views reflect your agency.

Does Scheduleistic support multiple workspaces?

Yes. The Agency plan includes 15 workspaces, and the Scale plan includes 50, each fully isolated.
