Quick answers

About the scheduler

Can Scheduleistic schedule posts across multiple channels?

Yes. Connect multiple networks per workspace and schedule them all from a single calendar, with one queue handling publishing.

Does Scheduleistic support multiple workspaces?

Yes. Every client or brand can have its own workspace with a separate calendar, channels, and media.

Is Scheduleistic self-hosted or cloud hosted?

Both. The scheduler can be self-hosted on your own VPS or run as a managed cloud service.
