Quick answers

Common questions about features

What is Scheduleistic?

Scheduleistic is social media scheduling software that helps agencies, creators, and small businesses plan, approve, and publish content across multiple channels from one clean workspace.

Is Scheduleistic built for teams?

Yes. Team roles and permissions let you invite teammates and clients with the right access per workspace, so collaboration stays organized.

Can Scheduleistic schedule posts across multiple channels?

Yes. You can connect and schedule LinkedIn, Facebook Pages, Instagram, Google Business Profile, YouTube, Pinterest, TikTok, Threads, Mastodon, Bluesky, and WordPress.
