FAQ

Clear answers before signup.

Who is Scheduleistic built for?

Agencies, freelancers, marketing teams, small businesses, creators, WordPress agencies, WooCommerce stores, and white-label SaaS resellers.

Does Scheduleistic publish automatically?

Scheduled posts are published by the queue after a user creates or approves them. AI suggestions remain drafts until reviewed and scheduled by a user.

Can clients approve posts?

Yes. Client approval workflows, comments, decisions, and notifications are implemented in the application.

Are all 13 networks production-ready?

The app includes 13 provider drivers. Real go-live still depends on each customer or operator completing provider credentials and app review requirements.
